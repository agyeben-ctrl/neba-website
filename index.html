Who We Are

An international practice
built on certified expertise

NEBA Group is a specialist IT governance, cybersecurity, and business advisory group operating across Australia and Ghana. We help organisations build stronger, more secure, and better-governed technology environments — and we have been doing so since 2012.

Our Australian practice, NABA EEA Pty Ltd, delivers GRC consulting, Essential Eight assessments, ISO 27001 ISMS implementation, and IT project and test management to clients across financial services, government supply chains, healthcare, and professional services in Sydney and nationally.

Our Ghana practice, NEBA Consult Ltd, offers the same IT governance and cybersecurity services to the West African market, complemented by accounting managed services, and real estate and agribusiness advisory — supporting businesses, diaspora investors, and international organisations operating in Ghana.

Both entities are led by the same founding principals, operate to the same professional standards, and carry the same certifications — giving clients in both markets access to consistent, credentialled expertise.

Bank of Ghana — Cyber & Information Security Directive (CISD)

The BoG CISD is a mandatory compliance framework applying to every Regulated Financial Institution (RFI) in Ghana — banks, microfinance, savings & loans, payment service providers, fintechs, digital credit, virtual asset providers, and more. Non-compliance attracts regulatory sanctions. NEBA Consult holds the exact credentials the directive names: CISA · CISM · ISO 27001 Lead Implementer · PMP · ISTQB — all explicitly listed in Annexure C of the directive as the recognised professional certifications for cybersecurity practitioners serving Ghana's financial sector.
